About LIFEFlow

LIFEFlow is a production-oriented Blood Bank Management and Hemovigilance Information System designed for blood transfusion centres, regional transfusion facilities, and affiliated hospitals.

Core Principles
  • Unit-Level Traceability: Every blood bag, apheresis donation, and separated component receives an immutable, globally unique tracking ID.
  • Cold Chain Assurance: Continuous physical and digital logging of storage devices (refrigerators, platelet incubators, deep freezers) with automated threshold excursion alarms.
  • Regulatory Hemovigilance: Full tracking of adverse events, return dispositions, quarantine investigations, and biohazard discard manifests with audit trails.
  • Concurrency Safety: Database-level atomic reservations guarantee that no two users or emergency requisitions can lock or issue the same physical unit simultaneously.
