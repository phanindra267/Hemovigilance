About RedLink

National Blood Bank Information Management & Hemovigilance System

Engineered for Safety, Speed, and Compliance

Standardized vein-to-vein hemovigilance workflow

RedLink is a production-oriented web application designed to support full-spectrum operations of regional blood transfusion services, component processing laboratories, hospital transfusion centers, and voluntary donor communities.

Unit-Level Traceability

Every phlebotomy donation bag, lab test aliquot, and separated blood component carries a unique alphanumeric identifier guaranteeing traceability from donor to recipient.

Cold Chain Governance

Real-time temperature logging across storage refrigerators (+2°C to +6°C), deep plasma freezers (-40°C to -80°C), and platelet agitators (+20°C to +24°C) with automatic excursion quarantining.

Release Gatekeeping

No blood unit can transition from quarantine to available inventory without verified non-reactive screening assays (HIV, HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria) signed off by a qualified Medical Officer.

Atomic Concurrency

Row-level database locking (select_for_update) ensures that simultaneous emergency hospital requisitions cannot reserve or issue the exact same physical blood unit.
